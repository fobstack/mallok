# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Argue about code, not
about people.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, unwelcome attention,
publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of
discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and anywhere
else someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/JasonYv/mallok/security/advisories/new),
which is the only private channel this repository has. Reports are handled
confidentially.

The maintainer may edit, delete or reject comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that do not follow this document, and may ban an account for
repeated or severe behaviour.
