We melt, roll, test and certify on one site. That is the whole reason the heat
number on your certificate means something: it points at a furnace we operate,
not at a broker's paperwork.

## What we supply

Commercially pure grades 1 and 2 for chemical process work, and Ti-6Al-4V and
Grade 9 for airframe, fastener and hydraulic applications. Stock sizes ship in
fifteen to twenty days; rolling to order adds about four weeks, and we say so
before you place the order rather than after.

## How we quote

Send the drawing and the tonnage. The engineer who prices it is the one who
will roll it, and they will tell you if a different grade would do the job for
less money.
