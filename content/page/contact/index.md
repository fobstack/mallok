Tell us the grade, the form and the quantity. If you have a drawing or a
specification, attach it — a quotation against a real drawing is worth more
than a price per kilogram.

[[inquiry]]

## Other ways to reach us

Enquiries are answered by the person who will handle the order, not by a
mailbox. If you would rather talk first, say so in the message and include a
time zone.
