Grade 2 is the workhorse of chemical process equipment: fully corrosion
resistant in seawater and most oxidising acids, readily formed and welded, and
far cheaper than the alloy grades.

## Forming and welding

Cold forming to a bend radius of 2.5 times thickness is routine. Welding is
straightforward with proper inert gas shielding on both sides of the joint.

## Pressure equipment

Plate for PED service ships with the additional documentation the directive
requires, including the impact test results where the design demands them.
