Grade 9 sits between commercially pure and Grade 5: stronger than the pure
grades, more formable than Ti-6Al-4V, and the standard choice for aircraft
hydraulic lines.

## Why it is used for hydraulics

The combination of strength and cold formability lets a line be bent to shape
without heat treatment, which matters when a hydraulic run has to follow a
structure rather than the other way round.
