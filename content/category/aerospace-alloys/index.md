Every alloy in this family is melted and rolled on the same site. Lead times
below assume stock; for non-stock sizes we roll to order, which adds roughly
four weeks.

Ultrasonic inspection to AMS-STD-2154 is available on bar above 12 mm and is
reported on the same 3.1 certificate as the chemistry and mechanical results.
