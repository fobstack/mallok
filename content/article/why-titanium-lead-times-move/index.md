title: Why titanium lead times move
description: Sponge supply, melt scheduling and the reason a quoted date can slip even when the mill is not busy.

A titanium lead time is mostly a melt scheduling problem, and melt scheduling
is mostly a sponge supply problem.

## Sponge comes first

Titanium sponge is produced by a handful of plants worldwide. A mill buys it
months ahead, and when sponge tightens, the mills that hold contracts keep
running while spot buyers wait. This is the single largest reason a quoted date
moves.

## Melts are batched

Vacuum arc remelting runs in campaigns by grade. If your order arrives just
after a Grade 5 campaign closes, the next one may be three weeks out — the
mill is not busy, it is simply not melting your alloy this week.

## What to do about it

Ask which campaign your material is scheduled into, not just for a date. A mill
that can name the campaign is telling you something real; one that only quotes
a date is telling you an average.
