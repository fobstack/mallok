Northbound Titanium is a fictional company created to demonstrate **Atelier**,
Mallok’s default theme for industrial and export-business websites.

## From material to conversation

Explore a catalogue of bar, plate and tube, compare sample specifications,
and follow related applications. The same content appears in product lists,
detail pages and category pages, with a consistent design throughout.

## Built for your own story

A real supplier can replace these examples with its own products, facility
photographs, inspection documents and verified qualifications. The photographs
here are illustrative; sample specifications and case studies are not offers,
certifications or records of actual customer projects.

## Made with Mallok

This bilingual demonstration uses Mallok’s static build and the bundled Atelier
theme. A deployed Mallok site can also include the content admin and inquiry
plugin. This read-only demo does not collect inquiries.
