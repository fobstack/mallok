A useful inquiry starts with the material form, grade, dimensions and quantity.
A drawing or specification helps the supplier understand the project.

## This is a demonstration

Northbound Titanium is fictional. This read-only site does not accept purchase
requests, drawings or personal contact details. No message will be sent.

On your own Mallok site, the inquiry plugin can connect a contact form to
Turnstile and Resend. Configure and test those services before accepting inquiries.
