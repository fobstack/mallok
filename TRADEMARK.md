# Trademark policy

The Mallok and Fobstack names and logos are trademarks of the Fobstack
project. The Apache-2.0 licence covers the **code**; it does not grant rights
to the names or marks (see section 6 of the licence).

This policy exists so people can tell an official release from someone else's
fork — not to restrict what you may do with the software. The licence already
answers that: you may use, modify, distribute and sell it.

## You do not need permission to

- Use the software, commercially or otherwise, under the Apache-2.0 licence.
- Say your site or product **"is built with Mallok"**, **"runs on Mallok"** or
  **"is Mallok-compatible"**, as a plain factual statement.
- Write about, review, teach or criticise Mallok, using the name to refer to
  it.
- Redistribute unmodified copies, keeping the name and the notices intact.
- Fork the code and keep the name **inside** the source: file names, package
  identifiers, comments, and attribution such as "based on Mallok".

## Please ask first before

- Naming a product, service, company or domain in a way that suggests it is
  official or endorsed — `mallok-hosting.com`, "Mallok Pro", "Mallok Cloud".
- Publishing a **modified** build under the name "Mallok" without saying it is
  modified. Distributing a fork that behaves differently under the same name
  is how a project's reputation gets spent by someone else.
- Using the logo as your own product's mark, or altering it.
- Using the name in a way that implies a partnership that does not exist.

## Naming a fork

Give it your own name and say what it came from:

> **Acme Sites** — a fork of [Mallok](https://github.com/fobstack/mallok)

That is accurate, needs no permission, and is clearer for your users than a
name they might mistake for the original.

## The "Powered by Mallok" link

The official themes include a `Runs on Mallok` link in the footer. **You may
remove it.** It is not a licence condition and never will be — a footer credit
that cannot be removed is an attribution requirement in disguise.

## Questions

Open a discussion at <https://github.com/fobstack/mallok>. Trademark questions
are rarely urgent and almost always resolvable by making the relationship
explicit.
